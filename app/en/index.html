Suite FR

Piles

Axial bearing capacity of a pile and of a group under a rigid cap, negative skin friction and simple settlement (EN 1997), in 1st or 2nd generation. Transverse tab: single pile under horizontal load, EN 1997-3 p-y springs (C.12).

Not covered: group effect under transverse load, piled raft, cyclic loading, CPT or pressuremeter methods (qb and qs are input), structural resistance of the shaft (to be checked separately, forces are provided).

Pile

Uncracked C30/37 concrete, D = 0.6 m: EI = 30 GPa × π·D⁴/64 ≈ 1.91·10⁵ kN·m². Free input (cracking, steel tube…).

Group under rigid cap

η = 1 − (θ/90)·[(m−1)n + (n−1)m]/(mn), θ = arctan(D/s). Empirical formula, outside the Eurocode.
